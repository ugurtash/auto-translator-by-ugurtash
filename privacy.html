Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Auto-Translator by UgurTash is a browser extension that translates text selected by the user.

Information processed

The extension processes the text selected by the user in order to provide a translation. The selected text and translation request may be sent to the translation service used by the extension.

Settings and usage

The extension stores settings such as the selected target language locally in the browser. Usage information may be processed by the service backend to enforce the free daily usage limit and subscription entitlement.

Payments

Paid subscriptions are handled by the payment provider used by the service. Payment card details are not stored by the browser extension.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the contact information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or service architecture changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

Founder sign-in

Optional founder sign-in sends your email and password over HTTPS to Supabase Auth through the project’s account service. Passwords are not saved by the extension. Session tokens are stored in trusted extension storage, inaccessible to website content scripts. Founder translation requests pass through Supabase to Google’s translation service. Signing out removes the stored session and requests server sign-out.
